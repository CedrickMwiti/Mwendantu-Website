What We Do

Community Initiatives

Outreach

Outside Broadcasts

We take our cameras and microphones to county events, church services, school functions and market days across every ward.

Education

School Partnerships

Our team visits local schools to mentor young people interested in journalism, broadcasting and media production.

Health

Public Health Drives

99.5 FM partners with county health officials to spread awareness on health campaigns and immunisation drives.

Culture

Cultural Programming

We celebrate Tharaka-Nithi's culture and heritage through dedicated features, music and storytelling segments.

Farmers

Farmers' Forum

A weekly radio segment connecting farmers with agricultural officers, market prices and weather updates.

Youth

Youth Voice

A platform for young people across the county to discuss the issues that matter most to their generation.
