Riiri Wetu — our pride. The story of Chuka's own television and radio station.

Our Story

Built in Chuka, for Tharaka-Nithi

Mwendantu Media began with a simple idea: Tharaka-Nithi County deserves a broadcaster that speaks directly to its people. Today, our television station and 99.5 FM radio reach households and businesses across Chuka, Chogoria, Magumoni and beyond — carrying news, music, talk and community programming every day.

We believe local stories deserve a local platform. From county government updates to school events, church services to market days, Mwendantu Media exists to reflect the life of Tharaka-Nithi back to itself.
